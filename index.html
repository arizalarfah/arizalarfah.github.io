Arizal is an experienced lecturer in the Cybersecurity department at the National Cyber and Cryptographic Polytechnic, a Service College organized by the National Cyber and Crypto Agency (BSSN) and has the task of organizing vocational education in the field of cyber security and cryptography. He was born in the city of Makassar, Indonesia, and received a master's degree from Hasanuddin University majoring in electrical engineering.

he has pursued a career as a lecturer for more than 10 years at several leading universities in Indonesia and is currently also very active in research and as a practitioner in the field of cybersecurity and ICT. His research concentrates mainly on cybersecurity, digital forensics, information systems, software engineering, hardware security, and IoT.

As an academic and practitioner, Arizal continues to increase his knowledge and competence so that he can inspire and provide innovation to new generations of students and the general public to be able to achieve success in their academic and professional careers.
